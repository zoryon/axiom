# AXIOM — Year 01 Curriculum Map

Year 01 is deliberately broader than a bootcamp. It combines the mathematical/scientific foundation expected in computing and engineering degrees with intensive systems-oriented programming practice.

The course catalog contains **11 courses**, **73 modules**, and **300 planned lesson IDs**. Full lesson prose and practice are authored separately later as `.lesson.md` files.

## Progression logic

The year starts with C, calculus, linear algebra, discrete mathematics, physics and professional tooling in parallel. C exposes the machine model early. Discrete mathematics supplies proof/Boolean foundations. Linear algebra and calculus prepare numerical work. The second term then deepens C into dynamic memory and data structures, introduces Rust after the learner has seen concrete C failure modes, begins numerical computing, and connects Boolean reasoning to digital logic and a simple processor model.

Engineering Laboratory I runs across the year and deliberately depends on material from the other courses. It is where specifications, debugging, testing, code review, performance measurement and technical communication are practiced as engineering behavior rather than isolated theory.

## What is intentionally deferred to later years

Year 01 does not try to compress the whole degree. Later years should add, among other subjects: multivariable analysis/advanced probability, algorithms as a dedicated course, computer architecture/assembly in greater depth, operating systems, networking, databases, circuit theory/electronics, signals, control systems, software engineering, theory of computation, compilers, security, distributed systems and advanced systems engineering.

## Reference basis

The map is a synthesis rather than a copy of one degree program. It was designed against current university-style foundations (including the 2026/27 Computer Engineering plan at the University of Florence) and modern CS curricular knowledge areas from ACM/IEEE-CS/AAAI CS2023, while adding more deliberate C/Rust/Linux/debugging practice than a typical academic first year.
